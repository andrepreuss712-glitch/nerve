# Phase 04.14 Plan 01: CRM Data Foundation Summary

**One-liner:** CrmNote DB model + customer_success_service with Top/Aktiv/Ruhig/Churn-Risiko badges from ConversationLog aggregation and DSGVO-compliant follow-up hints.

## What Was Built

CRM data foundation consisting of two artifacts:

1. **CrmNote model** (`database/models.py`) — new SQLAlchemy table `crm_notes` with unique user_id FK, free-text `notiz` field, and auto-updating `updated_at` via `onupdate=utcnow`. Auto-created on next app restart via `Base.metadata.create_all`.

2. **customer_success_service.py** — pure DB aggregation service (no LLM calls) with:
   - `get_all_user_crm_data(db)`: queries ConversationLog stats for last 30 days, computes status badges for all non-superadmin users, joins CrmNote data. Returns aggregated metrics only (no raw transcript text).
   - `_compute_status()`: implements D-01/D-02 thresholds — Top (above-avg calls + kb>60), Churn (14d no call AND no login), Ruhig (7d no call), Aktiv (else).
   - `get_followup_hints(users_crm)`: generates urgency-sorted hints for Churn (high) and Ruhig (medium) users, plus D-10 performance hints for low KB-score and low Einwand-Erfolgsrate.
