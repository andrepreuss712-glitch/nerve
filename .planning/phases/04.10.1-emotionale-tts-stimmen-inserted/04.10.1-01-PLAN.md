Purpose: Sales reps should hear the customer's mood shift in the voice -- agitation, warmth, tension -- like in a real call. This is the core training value of emotional TTS.

Output: Extended text_to_speech() with optional voice_settings, MOOD_VOICE_ZONES mapping dict, mood_to_voice_settings() function, secretary presets in SEKRETAERIN_TYPES, wired at all 3 TTS call sites.
