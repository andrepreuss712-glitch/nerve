### Task 1: /api/session-rating (app_routes.py Z.901-929)

### Task 2: /api/health pipeline_status (app_routes.py Z.1415-1426 + anonymization.py Z.365-386)

**get_pipeline_status() Rueckgabewerte (anonymization.py Z.365):**
- `'unavailable'`: is_pipeline_healthy=False (spaCy nicht ladbar, D-08 Kat. A)
- `'degraded'`: Rolling-Error-Rate > ROLLING_ERROR_THRESHOLD in ROLLING_ERROR_WINDOW_S (D-08 Kat. C)
- `'ok'`: Pipeline verfuegbar, keine erhoehlte Fehlerrate

**Implementierung:**
- Liest `_error_timestamps` direkt mit `_error_lock` (Thread-safe)
- Kein separates Flag fuer Kat. C noetig — berechnet aus vorhandener Rolling-Error-Liste
- Fehler-Fallback in api_health(): `pipeline_status = 'unknown'` bei Import-Exception

**Erweiterter /api/health JSON-Response:**
```json
{
  "status": "ok",
  "backup_status": "ok|stale|missing",
  "backup_age_hours": 1.5,
  "pipeline_status": "ok|degraded|unavailable"
}
```
