## Was gebaut wurde

### services/live_session.py

- `keyword_matchers: dict` + `keyword_matchers_lock` — per-Session Registry
- `get_matcher(sid)` — lazy-init `EinwandKeywordMatcher` pro Session
- `drop_matcher(sid)` — entfernt Matcher bei Session-Ende (Memory-Leak-Schutz)
- `state['slot1_variant_busy_until'] = 0.0` — shared monotonic timestamp fuer Slot-1-Guard
- `state['mic_muted'] = False` — via `mute_mic` Socket-Event setzbar
- `reset_session()` setzt beide neuen Keys zurueck

### services/deepgram_service.py

- **Keyword-Match Branch** im `else` (Interim) von `_make_on_message`:
  - Mute-Guard: `ls.state['mic_muted']` -> `return` ohne Match
  - `ls.get_matcher(sid).match_with_dedup(text, einwaende)` — Dedup-geschuerzt
  - Bei Match: `sio.emit('keyword_einwand_match', {keyword, typ, profile_einwand, transcript_snippet})`
  - Slot-1-Guard via `ls.state['slot1_variant_busy_until']` unter `ls.state_lock`
  - Bei Slot-1 frei: `sio.start_background_task(streame_auto_variante, ...)` non-blocking
- **`_make_on_utterance_end(sid)`** — Factory fuer Deepgram UtteranceEnd-Handler
- **`_open_deepgram_connection`** — registriert `LiveTranscriptionEvents.UtteranceEnd`
- **`handle_stop_live_session`** — ruft `ls.drop_matcher(_sid)`
- **`handle_disconnect`** — ruft `ls.drop_matcher(_sid)`
- **`@sio.on('mute_mic')`** — setzt `ls.state['mic_muted']` via `state_lock`

### services/claude_service.py (Lock-Unifikation, Wave 4 vorgezogen)

- `analyse_loop._variant_busy_until` (Funktions-Attribut) vollstaendig entfernt
- Ersetzt durch `ls.state.get('slot1_variant_busy_until', 0)` / `ls.state['slot1_variant_busy_until'] = _now + 6`
- Beide Spawn-Pfade (Keyword-Pipe in deepgram_service + analyse_loop in claude_service) teilen jetzt denselben Lock — kein doppelter Slot-1-Spawn moeglich
