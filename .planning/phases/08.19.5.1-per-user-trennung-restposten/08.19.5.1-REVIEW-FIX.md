### WR-02: `analyse_loop` phase classifier and cold-call inference reads/writes use global `ls.state`

**Files modified:** `services/claude_service.py`
**Commit:** d014392
**Applied fix:**
- Phase classifier block: replaced all `ls.state_lock` / `ls.state` reads (`current_phase`, `phase_change_count`, `_phase_cycle_at_last_change`, `mode`) with reads from `ls._session_state[sid]['state']` under `_session_state_lock`. Writes (`current_phase`, `current_phase_name`, `phase_changed_at`, `phase_change_count`, `_phase_cycle_at_last_change`, `phase_confidence`) now go to `_session_state[sid]['state']` under the same lock. A `_sid_phase_st2 is not None` guard prevents writing to a concurrently-removed SID and sets `phase_did_change = False` safely in that case.
- Cold-call inference block: replaced `ls.state_lock` / `ls.state` reads (`mode`, `current_phase`) and write (`cold_call_inference`) with per-SID reads/writes under `_session_state_lock`, with a `_sid_cc_target is not None` guard on write.

**Note:** `phasen_log` and `covered_phases` propagation still use module-level globals (`ls.phasen_log_lock`, `ls.covered_phases_lock`) — this coexistence is intentional per the HIGH-2 coexistence note in `init_session_state` and is out of scope for this phase.
